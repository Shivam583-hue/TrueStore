# TrueStore Code of Conduct

TrueStore welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone
participating in the project is expected to help keep its spaces respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other people with consideration and respect.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about code and ideas.
- Accept feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Help newcomers participate without belittling their questions or experience.
- Keep disagreements focused on the technical issue and allow others to disengage.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks.
- Personal attacks, repeated insults, or deliberate disruption.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized content.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Continuing unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Retaliation against someone for making a good-faith report or helping resolve one.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other
community spaces. It also applies when someone explicitly represents TrueStore in public.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the project maintainer, [Shivam583-hue](https://github.com/Shivam583-hue), using a
private contact method linked from their profile. If a private method is unavailable,
open a repository issue asking for a confidential reporting channel. Do not include
incident details or identify the people involved in that public request.

In a private report, describe what happened, where it happened, and any relevant links
or records. Share only the personal information needed to understand the concern.

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, share details only as needed to address
the concern, and respect the reporter's privacy. Maintainers involved in a report should
step aside from reviewing it when another uninvolved maintainer is available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may ask someone to change their behavior, remove inappropriate content,
issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response
should reflect the severity, impact, context, and whether the behavior is repeated.
Serious misconduct may result in an immediate restriction without a prior warning.

Someone affected by a moderation decision may request a review through the same private
reporting channel and provide additional context. Everyone, including maintainers, is
expected to follow this code.
